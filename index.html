        Shield

        Torrents connect through the SOCKS5 proxy of your VPN provider, so peers see the VPN's address instead of yours. With the VPN app already on, everything goes through it and the shield is not needed. DHT and UDP trackers stay off, so torrents find fewer peers, and a magnet link needs an HTTP tracker.

        The app restarts to apply it. The address is saved unencrypted in the app settings.
